Figure S[X]. Examples of inversion alignments to chimpanzee used for polarization. SVbyEye shows alignments between GRCh38 (top) and mPanTro3 (bottom) across (A) the recurrent 8p23.1 inversion and (B) the single-event 17q21.31 inversion. Green and blue indicate forward and reverse alignments, respectively. Red dashed boxes indicate the inversion coordinates in GRCh38. The alignment in (A) indicates that the GRCh38 orientation is ancestral, whereas the alignment in (B) indicates that the GRCh38 orientation is derived.
