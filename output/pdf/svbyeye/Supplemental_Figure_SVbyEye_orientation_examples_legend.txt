Figure S[X]. Examples of inversion alignments to chimpanzee used for polarization. SVbyEye shows alignments between GRCh38 (top) and panTro6 (bottom) across (A) the recurrent 8p23.1 inversion and (B) the single-event 15q11.2-q13.1 inversion. Green and blue indicate forward and reverse alignments, respectively. Red dashed boxes indicate the inversion coordinates in GRCh38. The alignment in (A) indicates that the inversion is ancestral, whereas the alignment in (B) indicates that the inversion is derived.
